Logo

The emblem has no wordmark; set "THE FUEL" in live type (Bodoni Moda 600, letter-spacing .16em at nav size, .32em in the loader). Files in assets/brand/logo/:

  • emblem-two-tone.svg: master (caramel fill, brown line).
  • emblem-mono-currentColor.svg: used as a CSS mask so it takes any colour (nav, footer, map pin).
  • emblem-fill-only-traced.svg + emblem-line-only-traced.svg: stacked to animate a "pour" fill in the loader and the Visit section.

Do not rely on the logo printed on the cup in the hero video; it blurs. The live type and emblem are overlaid in HTML.

Voice & copy

Short, sensory and confident. Lowercase playfulness in the big statement, sentence case elsewhere. A display headline is usually a roman phrase finished by an italic second half ("Pulled slow.", "Hidden in DHA.").

Shape & layout

The arch

Signature motif from the café's windows: border-radius: 999px 999px 12–20px 12–20px on image frames, cards, date chips and the hero video window. Mix arches with plain 18px-radius frames to keep rhythm.

Radii

Shadows

nav 0 10px 40px rgba(39,4,2,.14) · card hover 0 30px 60px -30px rgba(39,4,2,.35) · dialog 0 40px 100px -30px rgba(39,4,2,.6)

Grid

Max content width 1296px (wide 1456–1600px). Side padding clamp(16px, 5vw, 72px). Section padding clamp(80px, 10vw, 160px). Breakpoints: phone < 768, tablet 768–1023, desktop ≥ 1024.

Hit targets are at least 44px. Glass surfaces (nav, pill bar, phone dock) use var(--glass) + backdrop-filter: blur(18px) saturate(140%).

Motion

Signature easing cubic-bezier(.16,1,.3,1) (out-expo). Curtains use cubic-bezier(.7,0,.2,1). Everything collapses to static under prefers-reduced-motion: reduce, and video is skipped under Save-Data.

Variant B scroll theatre

One rAF loop reads each pinned section's progress p = -rect.top / (height - innerHeight) and writes styles directly. No smooth-scroll library is used, on purpose, so trackpads and phones don't get trapped scrolling.

  • 01 Hero (210vh, sticky): the video sits in an arch window clip-path that grows to full bleed; text fades and lifts; on desktop and tablet the video is scrubbed by scroll (blob-loaded mp4, falls back to a loop if a seek stalls for more than 600 ms). Phones play a plain loop. The poster always sits under the video.
  • 02 Statement (170vh): words go from 16% to 100% opacity in reading order.
  • 03 Day (260vh): clock hands run 8 AM → 2 AM, the progress ring fills, the background interpolates #FBF6EE → #F3E6D6 → #E29A64 → #592D14 → #270402, and the ink flips at luminance .5.
  • 04 Worlds (440vh): six steps of 1/6 each; palette morph as above; the arch image scales 1.04→1.09 and rotates ±2°.
  • 05 Best sellers (220vh): the card track slides right→left over a caramel glow, a 72px line grid and three nested arch outlines.
  • 06 Room tour (280vh): a horizontal track with per-item vertical drift (data-speed).
  • 07 Campaign: clip-path wipe left→right, image scales 1.15→1.
  • A fixed section counter "01 / 10 · name" sits bottom-left (not on phones). The nav turns dark over dark scenes.

Shared components

Nav (Fuel Nav.dc.html)

Desktop and tablet: a floating 64px glass pill, top 16px, max-width 1296px. It holds the emblem with a scroll-progress ring, links (Home, Menu, Our Space, Visit), the open-now chip (≥1200px), theme toggle, Reserve and the bag with a count badge. Tablet uses a "Menu" sheet. Phone: a 56px top bar (logo, Reserve, bag) plus a 68px bottom dock with 5 slots. The active link is scroll-spied from [data-spy="menu|space|visit"] at 45% of the viewport height. Overlays: bag drawer, reservation dialog, toast.

Bag

A right drawer, 460px wide (bottom sheet on phones), saved in localStorage['fuel-bag'] as [{name, world, qty}]. Any page adds through dispatchEvent(new CustomEvent('fuel-add', {detail:{add, world}})). Fields: qty ±, Pickup / Dine in, time (ASAP / 15 / 30 min), name (required), phone (≥10 digits). Shows a foodpanda link and a success state.

Reservation

Opened by fuel-reserve event. Fields:

  • date: the next 14 days, shown as arch chips
  • time: four time-of-day tabs (Morning, Afternoon, Evening, Late night) with 30-minute slots from 8:00 AM to 1:00 AM; slots less than 30 minutes ahead are struck through
  • guests: 1–12 (above 8 shows a "we call to confirm" note)
  • seating: Any / Swing seats / Window / Counter
  • name, WhatsApp number, optional note

Validation errors appear under each field. Success shows a summary.

Visit (Fuel Visit.dc.html)

Caramel section: address, live open status, Reserve CTA, foodpanda / WhatsApp / Call, directions. The map is a facade (pin card) that loads the Google embed 2400px before it scrolls into view and fades in once loaded.

Footer

Dark: emblem rule, giant THE FUEL wordmark, links, tagline and hours bar. Bottom padding is 120px on phones to clear the dock.

Open status

Computed from Intl.DateTimeFormat(..., {timeZone:'Asia/Karachi'}): open if hour ≥ 8 or < 2. Updates every 20–30 s. Chip text "Open now" or "Opens at 8 AM".

Build notes

  • The prototypes are references, not production code. They are "Design Component" files (*.dc.html) run by support.js: a template with {{ holes }} plus a small logic class per file. Recreate them in your stack (Next.js / Astro + React recommended) and treat the logic classes as behaviour specs.
  • To view them, serve the prototype/ folder over HTTP (e.g. npx serve prototype) and open Fuel Home B.dc.html. Opening them as file:// breaks the dynamic imports and fetches.
  • Pure helpers to port directly: fuel-theme.js (tokens, worlds, Lahore time, transitions), fuel-boot.js (pre-paint theme, view-transition CSS, route arrival), fuel-cursor.js (cursor).
  • Cross-page transitions use @view-transition { navigation: auto }. Keep the nav in its own named transition layer.
  • Images: serve AVIF/WebP with responsive srcset. Originals are around 1280×1600 and the LOW-RES story images about 640×1136, so never use those full-bleed on desktop. Lazy-load everything below the fold except the B room-tour images (they sit off-screen horizontally).
  • The hero video is a 6.5 s seamless muted loop (mp4 + webm, 1280×720). For scrubbing, re-encode with a keyframe every frame (-g 1) or use an image sequence.
  • Accessibility: skip link, aria-current on nav, dialogs with aria-modal and Escape to close, live status regions, focus ring #E29732, reduced-motion fallbacks for every pinned scene.
